# Privacy Policy - Dua Shorts Uploader

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Dua Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of this repository to upload original short videos (duas and adhkar) to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the owner's own channel (scope: youtube.upload).
- The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of any other user.
- The only credentials used are the owner's own OAuth token, stored as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret.
- No analytics, cookies or tracking are used.
